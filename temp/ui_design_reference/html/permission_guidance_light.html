Enable Accessibility

Thraksha Guardian requires Accessibility Services to monitor your device for security threats and automate protective actions silently in the background.

How to enable

1

Open Settings

Tap the button below to open your device settings.

2

Find Thraksha Guardian

Locate us under the 'Downloaded Apps' or 'Installed Services' section.

3

Toggle to 'On'

Switch the permission on to activate silent protection.
